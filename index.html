What We Do

EU Work Permit Consulting

We provide professional consultations on EU work permit options for international workers with different levels of skills and professional experience. Our consultations cover low-skilled, medium-skilled and highly skilled employment, including specific guidance for truck and lorry drivers interested in working in the European Union.

We explain the general requirements, available options, relevant considerations and potential challenges associated with working in different EU countries. Our role is to provide clear and practical information so clients can better understand their options and make informed decisions about their professional plans in Europe.

Education Consulting in the EU

We provide educational consulting for students interested in studying in the European Union. Our consultations cover different types of educational institutions, including universities, colleges and other recognised institutions, as well as the factors students should consider when choosing a study destination and programme.

We discuss admission requirements, educational pathways and other relevant considerations based on the student's individual goals and circumstances. Our aim is to give students a clear understanding of their available options so they can make informed decisions about their education in Europe.

Business & Investment Consulting in the EU

We provide consultations on business investment opportunities in EU countries for entrepreneurs and private investors considering entering the European market. Our consultations may cover investment in an existing business, establishing a new business, acquiring a company and selected property-related investments.

We discuss the relevant business environment, general regulatory considerations, potential risks and other factors that should be evaluated before making an investment decision. Our role is to provide objective information and professional guidance, helping clients better understand the opportunities and considerations associated with business investments in the EU.

EU Business & Residence Consulting

We provide consultations for entrepreneurs and investors who are considering establishing or acquiring an EU-based company and exploring residence options connected with business activity. We explain the general principles and requirements related to company ownership, business activity and Temporary Residence Permit (TRP) options in relevant EU countries.

Our consultations help clients understand the relationship between their intended business activities and applicable residence requirements, as well as the key factors to consider before proceeding. We provide information and strategic guidance, while the actual applications and legal procedures are handled by the relevant authorities and, where required, qualified professionals.

Looking for a Reliable EU Partner?

We are open to cooperation with recruitment agencies, educational agencies and other professional organisations from non-EU countries interested in developing reliable partnerships in the European market. We work with international partners who are looking for a trusted EU-based organisation to discuss opportunities related to employment, education, business and investment in the European Union.

Our role is to provide professional consulting, local market insight and a structured point of contact within the EU, helping our partners better understand available opportunities, requirements and potential areas of cooperation. We are interested in building long-term, transparent and mutually beneficial B2B relationships rather than one-off transactions.

If your organisation works with candidates, students, entrepreneurs or investors interested in Europe, we would be happy to discuss potential areas of cooperation and explore how our expertise and EU-based presence can complement your services.
